SOL Studio

PRIVATEAug 2026 — Present · ~2,000 commits · In production company-wide

Doctor content production system for SOL Integrative Wellness Centre: one sentence writes a full month of scripts → doctors film on a phone teleprompter → automatic rough cut → review → scheduled social publishing → one-click KPI reports. Used daily by non-technical staff across the company, with a Chinese / English / Malay interface.

Open the SOL Studio landing page ↗
  • Create Script: type “Dr Vig November” and AI writes the whole month in the doctor’s own voice, with hooks and medical-claims checks; also parses Marketing’s monthly PPT plans
  • Mobile filming app (iOS / Android, Expo): shot cards + scrolling teleprompter, with automatic upload and filing to the shared drive
  • AI auto rough cut: removes pauses and retakes, Whisper-aligned captions, B-roll matching, animated key figures, 3-second hooks, covers
  • Pass to AI: edit a video with one sentence; jobs queue to AI helpers on each Mac; exports CapCut project files
  • Browser timeline editor: multitrack, color grading, voice separation, background removal, transitions, 16 caption styles, and AI-cut to watch AI edit step by step
  • Multicam podcasts: cameras and mics auto-synced by audio, with automatic switching to whoever is speaking
  • Design board: AI writes FB / IG post copy; Auto Design produces three layered, editable PSD versions at once
  • Doctor AI voiceover: in-house cloned voices for narration; phone numbers read digit by digit, mixed English/Malay read as written
  • GHL scheduled publishing: FB / IG / TikTok / YouTube, with comment monitoring that alerts on new replies
  • Library of 3,000+ finished videos, dashboard, Content KPI Excel reports, webinars and client lists
  • Permissions and privacy: role/department access, chat tied to member identity, patient consent forms visible only to authorised staff
  • MCP server: Claude and other external AIs can create tasks, write scripts and read timelines directly in the app

Onset 3.0

PRIVATEStarted Nov 2026 · Full rewrite from scratch · In development

A personal studio app for everyday creators: scripting, filming, editing, covers and posts all in one place. Ask Onset, in the bottom-right of every screen, brings in AI help — and you can watch it work step by step and stop it anytime. Targets macOS, Windows, iOS and Android.

  • AI Onset first-run setup: a short Q&A sets your channel style, auto-organised into script / interview / design / editing styles (36 built in)
  • AI Script: five formats — short video, long-form, interview questions, podcast rundown, voiceover — sent straight to filming
  • Recording: webcam or external camera + teleprompter with adjustable speed and font
  • CapCut-style editor: keyframes, 39 transitions, masks, LUTs (incl. HLG / Apple Log / S-Log3 → 709), speed curves, stabilisation
  • Captions: Whisper speech-to-text, 20 caption styles, 13 fonts, karaoke word-by-word highlighting
  • Auto-cut and AI Edit: auto-removes pauses and retakes; AI edits chapter by chapter, every step shown live on the timeline
  • Audio: noise reduction, Demucs voice/background separation, AI voiceover, music and SFX, auto-ducking
  • Multicam podcasts and 2-hour+ long-form: auto-sync, speaker-based switching, chapter-by-chapter processing
  • Covers and image posts: 35 layouts, 44 design operations, for FB / IG / Xiaohongshu / TikTok / X / Threads
  • Export: MP4 / MOV / MP3 / WAV, plus CapCut, FCPXML, Premiere XML and FilmCraft project files
  • Animation mode: make videos without filming, using graphics, text animation and Godot 2D/3D
  • MCP: external AIs can drive the editor directly while Onset is open

PUBLIC · MIT2026-11

Built-in setup checks and one-click model download, AI prompt enhancement, character LoRAs, queueing and 2× upscaling; offloads work to the Neural Engine, cutting a 5-second video on an M3 Air from ~73 to ~35 minutes.
